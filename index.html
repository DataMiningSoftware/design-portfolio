Case Study — Transit Map

Problem: an existing map was geographically accurate but unreadable at a glance.

Approach: abstracted the network into a schematic using 45° angles, unified station markers, and a two-color palette.

Outcome: journey planning time dropped and the map was adopted across station signage and the app.
